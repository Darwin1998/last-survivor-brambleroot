# The Last Survivor Roadmap

## Completed vertical slice

- Milestone 1: large scrolling world, six biomes, fog of war, exploration, resources, survival statistics, day/time/weather, inventory capacity, versioned autosave, continue and safe backup loading.
- Milestone 2: Analyse as a proximity-based discovery mechanic, Online Shop with Credits/rank locks/confirmation, reusable XP and rank formulas, four tracked quests, one-time reward claiming, map and journal.
- Early Milestone 3: hunger, thirst, energy, temperature, untreated-water risk, consumable food/water, resource depletion and respawn, three crafting recipes.

## Next milestones

1. Add fishing, cooking, sleeping, injuries, spoilage, and tool durability.
2. Add placement-mode construction with partial builds: leaf shelter, firepit, water station, cabin.
3. Add farm plots, seeds, watering, crop growth and harvesting.
4. Add caves, wildlife, ore tiers, workstations and Rank 2+ catalog depth.
5. Add sound, accessibility settings, controller support and balance passes.
